# Related Publications

The following publications inform the research background and methodology of this POC.

---

## Primary References

**Anderson, L. W., & Krathwohl, D. R. (Eds.). (2001).**
*A Taxonomy for Learning, Teaching, and Assessing: A Revision of Bloom's Taxonomy of Educational Objectives.* Longman.

> Used as the framework for classifying curriculum expectations into pedagogical target levels. The `bloom_baseline_level` field in `InstitutionalProfile` draws directly from this taxonomy.

---

**Randall, A. K. C. (2024).**
A Synthesis Proposal: Merging AI in Education with Automata Theory.
*CEUR Workshop Proceedings, Vol. 3927 (EC-TEL 2024 Doctoral Consortium)*, 1–9.
https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-3927/paper14.pdf

> Domain anchor for the benchmark: the COMPSCI 250 "even-even" (EE) regular expression problem used in all 10 synthetic scenarios is discussed in Appendix B of this paper.

---

**Randall, A. K. C. (2026).**
AI Experiential Learning Lab US Fall 2026: Mid-Program Milestone Checkpoint (Team: Intelligent Tutors R Us).
*IBM SkillsBuild AI Experiential Learning Lab.*

> The IBM SkillsBuild milestone that directly preceded this Sprint One POC. The POC builds on the research direction established there.

---

**Randall, A. K. C., Ngo, J. V., Woolf, B. P., & Lan, A. (2025).**
Personalized Paths to Mastery: Real-Time Adaptive Feedback for Student Engagement in Discrete Mathematics.
*AAAI 2025 Workshop on AI for Education (AI4EDU).*
https://ai4ed.cc/workshops/aaai2025

> Prior work on adaptive feedback and Reusable Learning Objects in COMPSCI 250, which identified limits in automated correctness checking and motivated the curriculum-aware approach in this POC.

---

**Randall, A. K. C., Almutairi, R., Chu, K., Bhuiya, N., & Woolf, B. P. (2026).**
A Research to Practice of Privacy, Fairness, and Utility Tradeoffs in Federated Learning.
*IEEE SoutheastCon 2026*, 99–106.
https://doi.org/10.1109/SOUTHEASTCON63549.2026.11476664

> Demonstrates that improving one system measure does not necessarily improve all outcomes — a finding that motivates the cross-profile comparison approach used here.

---

**Woolf, B. P., Lane, H. C., Chaudhri, V. K., & Kolodner, J. L. (2013).**
AI Grand Challenges for Education.
*AI Magazine, 34*(4), 66–84.

> Seminal reference for challenges in AI learning systems, including the concern that systems may be built around narrow educational assumptions that do not transfer across contexts.
