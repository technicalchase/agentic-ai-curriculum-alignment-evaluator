## Course References

**University of Massachusetts Amherst, Manning College of Information and Computer Sciences.**  
*COMPSCI 250: Introduction to Computation.*

**Course Description:** An undergraduate core course in discrete mathematics covering logic, elementary number theory, mathematical induction, recursion on trees, search algorithms, finite state machines, and introductory computability.  The course includes weekly lectures, discussion sections, and hands-on labs that reinforce mathematical concepts through problem solving and practical exercises.

Course offerings referenced for the synthetic UMass profile:

- [Spring 2022](https://people.cs.umass.edu/~barring/cs250s22/)
- [Summer 2022](https://people.cs.umass.edu/~gparvini/)
- [Fall 2023](https://people.cs.umass.edu/~barring/cs250f23/)
- [Spring 2024](https://people.cs.umass.edu/~barring/cs250s24/)

The author served as a Teaching Assistant in four offerings of this course. The synthetic UMass profile draws loosely on its instructional expectations and common student difficulties. It does not reproduce official course documentation.
